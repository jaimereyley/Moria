This is a Moria vault. Each file in it is encrypted with Apple's own format, so you can get your files back without
Moria, on any Mac.

The easy way: double-click Recover, enter the vault's password, and choose where the files should go. If macOS
won't open it, right-click it and choose Open.

In Terminal, from this folder:

    read kdf rounds salt < key.kdf
    secret=$(printf %s 'YOUR PASSWORD' | openssl enc -aes-256-cbc -pbkdf2 -md sha256 -iter $rounds -S $salt -pass stdin -P | sed -n 's/^key=//p')
    key=$(aea decrypt -i key.aea -o /dev/stdout -password-value $secret | base64)
    mkdir ~/Desktop/Recovered
    for f in files/*.aea; do aa extract -i "$f" -d ~/Desktop/Recovered -key-value "base64:$key"; done

How it works: files/ holds one Apple Encrypted Archive per file or folder, recording its path. key.aea holds the
key they're encrypted with, itself encrypted with your password as stretched by the PBKDF2 settings in key.kdf.
